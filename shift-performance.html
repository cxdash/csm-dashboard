How this is calculated. Source: Gladly Agent Reporting sheet (daily buckets, Pacific Time). Each agent day is rebuilt into shifts. Shifts that cross midnight (e.g. 10 PM 10/16 → 6 AM 10/17) get split by Gladly into two calendar days. Wherever the sheet shows a day with no new login (the agent was still logged in from the night before), that time is joined back to the shift it belongs to, so each split shift counts once.
Regular shift = logged-in time within ±1 hr of the shift length (7–9 hrs for an 8-hr shift). OT / Offset = logged in more than shift + the OT buffer (default: more than 9 hrs). Short = under shift − 1 hr (half days, late log-ins, start or end of a schedule change). Short shifts are left out of the regular average, so they don't pull it down.
Avg per regular shift = total handled on regular shifts ÷ number of regular shifts. This is the actual daily average on a normal 8-hr day. OT shift avg = average handled on an OT day (the whole day, OT included). OT rate per 8 hrs = OT-day volume ÷ (OT-day hours ÷ 8). This answers whether the agent keeps the same pace during overtime. Pace vs regular = OT rate per 8 hrs compared with the regular-shift average. Emails = Contact Fulfilled – Email · Chats = Contact Fulfilled – Chat · Combined = Emails + Chats.
